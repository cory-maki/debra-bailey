About

Meet Dr. Bailey

A clinical psychologist in West Hartford, Connecticut, Debra Bailey, Ph.D., brings a lifelong curiosity about human behavior and an unwavering dedication to helping others heal. Her path into psychology was anything but ordinary.

Before entering the field of mental health, she spent her early years on the competitive equestrian circuit, training and showing Arabian horses across the United States and Canada — an experience that instilled a remarkable work ethic, emotional attunement, and patience that continue to shape her work in the consulting room.

Today her practice reflects rigorous training in both clinical and experimental psychology, years of crisis and inpatient work, and a relational philosophy built on being genuinely heard. You can read more about her background and training.
